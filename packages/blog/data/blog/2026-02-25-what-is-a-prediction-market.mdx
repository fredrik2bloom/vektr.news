![What is a prediction market?](https://www.tbstat.com/wp/uploads/2026/02/Prediction-Market-800x450.jpg)

Prediction markets are platforms that allow users to trade on the outcomes of real-world events. Participants buy and sell shares representing the potential outcomes of the event, with the implied probability of these outcomes shifting along with the price action of the shares.
