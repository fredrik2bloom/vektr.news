Grayscale Investments has named BNB (BNB) the largest holding in its Smart Contract Fund, handing the token a 30.6% weighting and pushing Ethereum (ETH) and Solana (SOL) out of the top spot. The shift landed in the asset manager’s second-quarter rebalance, announced August 5 and effective August 3.
