Vektr is an AI‑assisted, community‑curated crypto news platform. We aggregate trusted sources, de‑duplicate and normalize stories, and highlight credible reporting and real market signals—so you can scan the space fast without the noise.

Every article is treated like a vector with “slope” that reflects community momentum. Your votes nudge the slope: amplify what’s insightful, dampen what’s overstated. The result is a feed that adapts to what informed readers find useful right now.

## Methodology
import Image from '@/components/Image'

Vektr combines automated aggregation with community input. We fetch and normalize stories from trusted sources, de‑duplicate near‑identical items, summarize for fast scanning, and tag topics and quality signals. Readers then vote, adjusting each article’s slope, which influences its rank in the feed.
