Vektr is an AI‑assisted, community‑curated crypto news platform. We aggregate trusted sources, de‑duplicate and normalize stories, and highlight credible reporting and real market signals—so you can scan the space fast without the noise.

Every article is treated like a vector with “temperature” that reflects community momentum. Your votes nudge the slope: amplify what’s insightful, dampen what’s overstated. The result is a feed that adapts to what informed readers find useful right now.
